The Wrong Way

About

The idea

Two pins, one city, and a lot of tempting wrong turns. Draw the shortest route from A to B along the real streets. When you submit, the game runs Dijkstra's algorithm on the very same road graph and shows you the best way.

How to play

  • Click a ringed intersection next to your line's end, or press and drag along the streets.
  • Dragging back over the previous junction undoes a step. Roads can't be skipped and routes can't loop.
  • Ctrl/Cmd + Z undoes. Clear and Reset never touch the map or the pins.

Scoring

efficiency = optimal ÷ yours × 100 and points = 1000 × (efficiency ÷ 100)². Any route as short as the optimal one scores a perfect 1,000. Distances are in fictional metres, derived from the street geometry.

Modes

The Daily Challenge is one deterministic city per local day, identical on refresh, saved in this browser only. Practice generates endless new maps and keeps its own statistics. Nothing syncs between devices.
